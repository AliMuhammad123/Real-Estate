What Carry does while you look around

Four things run on their own, so nobody has to open a spreadsheet or wait on a callback.

Reprices everything

Move the rate, the down payment or your budget and all listings recalculate their full monthly carry in the same breath — including the mortgage insurance most calculators quietly skip.

Reads plain English

Type the way you'd tell a friend. The search bar pulls out beds, city, budget and must-haves, turns them into filters, and shows you exactly what it understood.

Watches the market

Leave the watch on and Carry keeps checking your search. Price cuts and fresh listings that fit your ceiling arrive as they happen, already priced against your numbers.

Books the viewing

Pick a slot and it confirms straight away, sends you a calendar file, scores how ready you are to buy, and writes the agent's brief so they arrive knowing your situation.

Carry is a working demo. Listings, agents and market events are simulated so you can try the whole flow. Figures are estimates for illustration, not a lending offer.
