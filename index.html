Demand price is not the price. Neither is the file rate.

Between the asking price and the day you get possession sit stamp duty, CVT, section 236K advance tax, registration, society transfer and the agent's one percent. Naqsha adds them up before you make an offer, not after.

What Naqsha does that a listings portal does not

Six things run on their own while you browse.

Checks the asking price

Every listing is divided down to a rate per marla and held against the going rate for that society. You see "18% above DHA Phase 6 rate", not just a number the agent typed in.

Scores the listing itself

Duplicate postings of the same property, months on the market with no price move, missing photographs, price on request, unverified agency. Each one pulls the trust score down and is named on the card.

Adds the real cost

Stamp duty, capital value tax, section 236K advance tax at your filer status, registration, society transfer and agent commission, totalled per listing before you commit to anything.

Speaks in marla and kanal

Switch between marla, kanal, square yards and square feet and the whole site converts at once, including the per-unit rates. Karachi in yards, Lahore in marla, no mental arithmetic.

Watches while you look

Leave the watch on and price cuts and fresh listings that fit your budget arrive as they happen, already checked against the area rate.

Books the visit

Pick a slot, and it confirms, writes the dealer a brief with your budget and financing position, and hands you a calendar file. No four-day WhatsApp thread to agree a time.
